Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the portal.

How do I create an account?＋

Click Get Started or Citizen Login, then choose Create Account and register using your email and password.

How do I submit a request?＋

Log in, enter your details, select a service, review the required documents, upload your files, then submit the appointment.

What files can I upload?＋

The portal accepts PDF files and images. Multiple files can be uploaded for one request.

How can I check my request status?＋

After logging in, your Request History & Status section shows your submitted requests and their latest status.

Can I cancel a request?＋

Pending requests can be cancelled from your dashboard. Requests that have already progressed may no longer show the cancel option.

I still have a question. What can I do?＋

Use the blue chatbot button in the lower-right corner for quick answers to common portal and service questions.
